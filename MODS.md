# Purenail's Silksong Mod List

Complete list of all mods authored by Purenail for Silksong.

# Invincibility Monitor

[InvincibilityMonitor](https://github.com/dplochcoder/Silksong.InvincibilityMonitor) is a Silksong port of Hollow Knight's [Invincibility Monitor](https://github.com/flibber-hk/HollowKnight.InvincibilityMonitor). It adds many options for making the player invincible at specific times during multiplayer.

# Mod Menu

[ModMenu](https://github.com/silksong-modding/Silksong.ModMenu) is the org-standard mod for implementing custom menus in Silksong. It is a community project primarily authored by me, [Flibber](https://github.com/flibber-hk), and [BadMagic](https://github.com/BadMagic100).

# Purenail Util

[PurenailUtil](https://github.com/dplochcoder/Silksong.PurenailUtil) is a library mod containing shared collection and other utilities for other mods.

# The Hunt Is On

[TheHuntIsOn](https://github.com/dplochcoder/Silksong.TheHuntIsOn) is an [SSMP](https://github.com/Extremelyd1/SSMP) add-on for [Speedrunner vs. Hunter races](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hzStATm-H2s) in Silksong.
